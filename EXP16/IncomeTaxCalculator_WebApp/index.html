Calculation method

  • Gross annual income = monthly gross pay × 12 + annual bonus + other taxable income.
  • Taxable income = gross annual income − annual HRA − standard deduction − eligible pension deduction − annual health insurance.
  • Pension deduction is capped at the configured maximum.
  • The brief says tax is nil below ₹5,00,000. This condition is applied before the slab formula.
